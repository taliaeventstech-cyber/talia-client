# TALIA Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the agreement between the customer identified in the applicable order form (Customer) and Andrea Pascual Giménez, trading as TALIA (TALIA), for use of the TALIA service.

## 1. Roles and scope

For Customer Content processed through chatbots, connected mailboxes, knowledge sources and supervised workflows:

- Customer is the controller or responsible party and determines the purposes and essential means of processing.
- TALIA is the processor, service provider or operator and processes Customer Personal Data only on documented Customer instructions.
- Each party remains an independent controller for the personal data it processes for its own account administration, billing, legal compliance and security purposes.

This terminology includes the equivalent roles under the GDPR, Brazil's LGPD and Mexico's LFPDPPP.

## 2. Processing details

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Providing TALIA's chatbot, email-agent, knowledge, analytics, support and security services |
| Duration | The subscription term plus the deletion and return period described below |
| Nature | Collection, hosting, organization, retrieval, transmission, generation, classification, support, deletion and de-identification |
| Purposes | Providing and securing the contracted services, measuring contracted usage and carrying out documented support instructions |
| Data subjects | Customer staff, website visitors, ticket buyers, correspondents, suppliers and other people whose data Customer submits |
| Data | Account identifiers, contact details, chat and email content, technical identifiers, feedback, knowledge content and usage metadata |
| Sensitive data | Not intended. Customer must not intentionally submit special-category or sensitive data unless separately agreed with appropriate safeguards |

## 3. TALIA obligations

TALIA shall:

1. Process Customer Personal Data only to provide the service and on documented Customer instructions, including lawful transfer instructions.
2. Ensure people authorized to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
3. Maintain proportionate technical and organizational security measures.
4. Assist Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, with access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and other applicable data-subject requests.
5. Assist Customer with security, breach notification, impact assessments and regulator consultations where applicable.
6. Notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
7. Delete or return Customer Personal Data at the end of service, unless law requires retention.
8. Make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance available to Customer and support proportionate audits no more than once annually, except after a material incident or regulator request.
9. Inform Customer if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law.

## 4. Customer obligations

Customer shall:

1. Provide lawful instructions and an applicable legal basis for processing.
2. Give website visitors, staff and correspondents the notices required in their jurisdiction.
3. Configure a valid privacy-policy URL in the TALIA chatbot.
4. Avoid using TALIA for prohibited, discriminatory, safety-critical or unlawful automated decisions.
5. Restrict access to authorized personnel and review permissions regularly.
6. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive data and handle data-subject requests as controller.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Chatbot conversation content is retained for 90 days after the last activity unless Customer deletes it earlier.
- Manual deletion removes content and immediately removes conversation identifiers from retained usage records.
- Chatbot usage quantities, tokens and costs may be retained without conversation/session identifiers for up to 12 months for billing and service reporting.
- Synced email content and generated drafts are retained for up to 90 days unless deleted earlier.
- Billing records are retained for the legally required accounting and tax period.

Backups may retain encrypted copies for their ordinary backup lifecycle. Restored data remains subject to the same deletion policy.

## 6. Subprocessors

Customer gives general authorization for the subprocessors listed in [`subprocessors.md`](subprocessors.md). TALIA shall impose data-protection obligations appropriate to the services each subprocessor provides and remains responsible for its processor obligations.

TALIA will provide reasonable advance notice of a material new subprocessor. Customer may object on substantiated data-protection grounds. The parties will work in good faith on a reasonable alternative; if none is available, either party may terminate the affected service.

## 7. International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred internationally:

- for EEA data, the parties incorporate the applicable controller-to-processor or processor-to-processor modules of the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), as required;
- for Brazilian data, the parties shall incorporate the standard contractual clauses approved by ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024 when that is the selected transfer mechanism;
- for Mexican data, the recipient shall assume confidentiality and data-protection obligations consistent with the privacy notice, Customer instructions and the LFPDPPP.

If this DPA conflicts with mandatory transfer clauses, the mandatory clauses prevail for the relevant transfer.

## 8. Security measures

TALIA's measures include, as applicable:

- encryption in transit and provider-level encryption at rest;
- encrypted OAuth credentials;
- tenant separation and role-based access controls;
- restricted and auditable support access;
- secrets management and credential rotation procedures;
- rate limiting, input validation and domain allowlists;
- incident response, vulnerability management and recovery procedures;
- data minimization and scheduled deletion controls;
- human supervision for email drafts and configurable chatbot fallbacks.

## 9. AI processing

TALIA may use AI subprocessors to generate, classify, retrieve and summarize content solely to provide the contracted service. Customer Content is not authorized for training general-purpose models. TALIA will identify interactive AI to end users and maintain reasonable records of the AI components used.

## 10. Liability and order of precedence

Liability is governed by the main service agreement unless mandatory law requires otherwise. This DPA prevails over conflicting service terms concerning processing of Customer Personal Data.
